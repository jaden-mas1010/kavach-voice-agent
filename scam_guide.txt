
KAVACH SCAM AWARENESS GUIDE

1. OTP and banking scams
Never share your OTP, banking password, or UPI PIN
with another person. A caller claiming to be from a
bank does not automatically prove their identity.
Verify requests through your bank's official app or
a contact method you already trust.

2. UPI payment scams
A UPI PIN is used to authorise payments, not to
receive money. Be cautious if someone asks you to
enter your PIN or scan a QR code to receive a refund
or prize.

3. Suspicious links
Check the actual domain before opening a link.
Shortened URLs, IP-address URLs, and lookalike
domains deserve extra caution, but are not always
malicious. When in doubt, open the official website
yourself instead of using the supplied link.

4. Remote access scams
Be cautious if an unexpected caller asks you to
install a remote-access app or share your screen.
Never give an unknown person control of your
device or access to banking sessions.

5. Prize and refund scams
Be suspicious of unexpected prizes or refunds that
require an upfront payment. Verify the offer through
the organisation's official channels before paying
or sharing information.

6. Job scams
Be cautious about unexpected job offers that demand
registration fees, interview fees, or payments to
guarantee employment. Independently verify the
employer and the vacancy.

7. General safety
A warning indicator does not prove a message is a
scam, and the absence of indicators does not prove
it is safe. Do not share passwords, OTPs, or PINs.
When uncertain, pause and verify independently.
